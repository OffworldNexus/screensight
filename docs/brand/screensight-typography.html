DEVICE RULES / 800 × 480

Read at a glance.
Primary data 37–80 px. Action titles ≥15 px. Small 10–12 px metadata is secondary, never the sole warning.

Touch, not mouse.
Controls ≥44 × 48 px; typically 58 px high. At this panel size that is about 8.5 × 8.6 mm. No hover-only meaning.

Ship & verify.
All six candidates are SIL OFL 1.1. Keep licences with binaries. Verify glyph coverage and rasterisation on the actual device.
